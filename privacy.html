Privacy policy

SiteCloseout is provided by Saksham Goyal. This policy describes the iPhone app and this support website.

Data stays in your local workspace

SiteCloseout stores inspection projects, notes, inspector details, photos, drawings, drafts and issued reports locally on your device. The app does not collect data for the developer and does not use accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics or a developer-operated upload service.

Photos and permissions

The camera is used when you choose to take an inspection photo. The system photo picker provides only the images you select. Original images may contain camera metadata, including capture time or location. The app retains original imports; markup creates a separate copy. Files providers or iCloud Photos may download selected content through the operating system.

Sharing and backups

You choose when and where to share reports and backups. PDFs contain project information, recorded finding history and included photos for the selected scope. Full backups include all projects, drafts, original attachments and reports. Review files before sharing. Files shared with another app or provider are handled under that destination's privacy practices. Backup files do not have additional password encryption.

Storage and deletion

Local workspace files use iOS Data Protection. iOS backups may include app data according to your device settings. SiteCloseout does not operate cloud synchronization.

Archiving preserves project data. This version retains original attachments and prior restored workspaces to protect inspection history. To remove all local app data, delete the app through iOS Settings. Delete copies stored outside the app separately. Keep an external backup before deleting the app or moving devices.

This website and support requests

This static website uses GitHub Pages. It adds no analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical information when hosting the site under its privacy statement.

Support uses GitHub Issues. Information you voluntarily post is public and may be used to respond to your request. Do not post private inspection records or personal information. GitHub controls account information and platform retention under its policies. App Store purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's policies.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the support page and avoid including confidential information in a public request. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with a new effective date.
