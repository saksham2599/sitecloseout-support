On-site inspection · iPhone

Follow the defect.
Check the repair.

Keep photos, drawing locations and return visits together in one offline inspection workspace.

For individual inspectors and site professionals who need a clear record of what was reported, what changed and what was checked.

From first finding to verified closure

Capture a defect with its room, trade and exact location. Save and continue to the next finding. Return to the same reference on later visits, with a history of observations and evidence.

A reported repair stays outstanding until you verify it with a photo, inspector name and on-site check.

Keep the drawing and report in context

Pin defects to PDF or image drawings. Confirm locations when a drawing revision changes. Issue fixed PDF and CSV reports for the whole project or a trade, with explicit visit coverage.

Your workspace stays with you

No account, ads, analytics or developer-operated cloud service. Save full workspace backups to a location you choose. SiteCloseout does not provide team sync, building certification or independently authenticated evidence.

Designed for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. App Store availability will follow review and release.
