Help & getting started

Keep your inspection moving.

Start a project

Create a project, then add a defect with a title, room, exact location and note. You can also explore the clearly labelled sample project. Add photos from Camera, Photos or Files; markup creates a separate image while keeping the original.

Capture several findings

Use Save & add another to move to a clean capture. Room and trade carry forward. Recent wording can help start a similar finding; evidence and closure status are never copied.

Return to the site

Start a revisit and record new findings against existing defect references. Choose Continue inspection to work through a room or trade, record today’s finding and advance. Pause and resume at your saved position. Moving between items does not mark them inspected. Compare the original finding with any later evidence, dates and captions. A reported repair remains outstanding until verified.

Verify a repair

Enter your inspector name in Settings, attach an evidence photo and affirm the on-site check. If the defect uses a drawing, confirm its location on the current revision.

Issue a report

Finish visit shows changes and remaining work. In Reports, choose the whole project or one trade. Keep full history, or enable Visit update report and choose an earlier visit. Updates include the last finding at or before that visit and later findings for changed defects, plus a current coverage register of every scoped defect. Issue a PDF and CSV. Issued reports remain fixed when the workspace changes. Review the included information before sharing.

Back up or move your work

In Settings, create a full backup and save the shared .sitecloseout file outside the app. Creating an export does not prove that it was saved. Use Verify a saved backup to check a selected file without replacing the workspace, and see whether its content still matches. Restore is separate: it validates the backup, then replaces the active workspace; it does not merge projects. Keep a backup before deleting the app or moving devices. Backup files have no additional password encryption.

Camera or attachment trouble

For denied camera permission, open iOS Settings and enable Camera for SiteCloseout. Images stored in iCloud may need to download before offline use. If an attachment is unreadable, preserve your workspace and restore a verified backup. Report generation is blocked when an included photo is unreadable.

Contact support

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your iOS version, SiteCloseout version and steps to reproduce the issue. A free GitHub account is required.

Support requests are public. Do not include client names, site addresses, personal contact details, photographs, drawings, reports or backup files. Describe the issue without confidential inspection data.
